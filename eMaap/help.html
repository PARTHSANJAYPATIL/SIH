Help & Support

Find answers to common questions

Getting Started

How do I create an account?

Click on "Register Account" from the home page. Select your stakeholder type (User, LMO, GATC, or Admin), fill in your details, and submit. You'll receive a confirmation and can login immediately.

What documents do I need to register?

For users: GSTIN (if applicable), business address, and contact details. For LMOs/GATCs: Official credentials and designation. For admins: Government authentication.

Is eMaap free to use?

The platform is free. However, verification fees apply as per the Legal Metrology Act, 2009 rules, which vary based on instrument type.

Applying for Verification

How do I apply for initial verification?

1. Register your instrument in the system with details like make, model, serial number, capacity, and accuracy.
2. Submit an application for "Initial Verification".
3. Pay the prescribed fee using UPI or online payment.
4. Schedule an inspection slot.

How long is a verification certificate valid?

Validity period depends on instrument type, typically 12 months for weighbridges and flow meters, 24 months for water meters and length measures.

Can I schedule inspection at a convenient time?

Yes. After application submission, you can choose from available time slots displayed on the scheduling page. LMOs/GATCs will confirm the slot.

What if my application is rejected?

If your instrument fails inspection, you'll be notified with the reason. You can rectify the issue and resubmit for re-inspection within 30 days without additional fees.

Certificates & Verification

How do I get my digital certificate?

After successful inspection, a digital verification certificate is generated automatically and made available in your "Certificates" section. You can download and print it.

How do I verify if a certificate is authentic?

Use the "Verify Certificate" page. Enter the certificate ID, instrument ID, or scan the QR code. The system will validate and display all details.

Will I receive renewal reminders?

Yes. Automated email and SMS reminders are sent 30 days and 7 days before certificate expiry, with a direct link to apply for re-verification.

For LMOs & GATCs

How do I access the verification queue?

After login, go to "Verification Queue" in your dashboard. You'll see submitted applications awaiting scheduling.

Can I record inspections offline?

Yes. The Field App is designed to work offline. Fill inspection details and upload photos locally. They sync to the server when network is available.

How do I issue a digital certificate?

After recording a "Pass" result in field inspection, a certificate is auto-generated with a unique ID and QR code. You can review, print, and share it digitally.

Technical Support

What browsers are supported?

Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge (latest versions). Mobile browsers on iOS and Android are fully supported.

Is my data secure?

Yes. The platform uses HTTPS encryption, role-based access control, and follows government security guidelines for sensitive data.

What do I do if I forget my password?

Click "Forgot Password?" on the login page. Enter your registered email. You'll receive a password reset link within 5 minutes.

How do I report a bug or issue?

Email us at support@emaap.india.gov.in with a detailed description and screenshot. Our team will respond within 24 hours.
